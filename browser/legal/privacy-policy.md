# **Privacy Policy for gib.gg**

**Last Updated:** February 19, 2026

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how **gib.gg** (“we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and protects information when you use our website, APIs, and related services (collectively, the “Service”).

By using the Service, you agree to the practices described here.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### **2.1 Information You Provide**

* **Account Information:** email address, username, authentication data.
* **Uploaded Content:** WAD files, metadata, descriptions, images, and any other materials you upload.
* **Communications:** support requests, feedback, or messages you send to us.

### **2.2 Information We Collect Automatically**

* **Log Data:** IP address, browser type, operating system, device info, pages visited, timestamps.
* **Cookies & Local Storage:** used for authentication, session continuity, and preferences.
* **Usage Data:** interactions with the Service, upload behavior, search activity, and error diagnostics.

### **2.3 Third-Party Data**

If you authenticate through a third-party identity provider (e.g., OAuth services), we may receive your email and profile identifiers from that provider.

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information for:

* **Service operation:** authentication, account management, file hosting, and content delivery.
* **Security & Abuse Prevention:** detecting fraud, spam, or malicious uploads.
* **Performance & Reliability:** debugging, monitoring, analytics, and improving system performance.
* **Feature Development:** improving search, metadata, curation, and community tools.
* **Legal Compliance:** responding to valid requests, DMCA notices, and preventing illegal activity.

We do **not** sell your personal information.

---

## 4. How We Share Information

We may share information with:

* **Service Providers:** hosting, object storage (e.g., S3-compatible storage), analytics, and email providers.
* **Legal Authorities:** when required by law or subpoena, or to protect the Service from abuse.
* **Other Users:** when you publish a WAD or profile publicly, associated metadata becomes publicly visible.

We do **not** share personal data with advertisers or data brokers.

---

## 5. Data Storage & Security

* Data is stored on industry-standard cloud infrastructure.
* Access to personal data is restricted to essential operations.
* Encryption is used for data in transit and at rest where supported by infrastructure.

No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data.
